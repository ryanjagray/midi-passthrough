Midi Passthrough

A simple macOS utility that passes MIDI from one device to another.

What it does

Choose a MIDI input and a MIDI output, and Midi Passthrough forwards everything between them with channels left intact. You can filter out notes, clock, control change (CC) and program change (PC) messages, and 16 indicators show activity on each channel. Your choices are remembered, and the app reconnects automatically when a device is unplugged and plugged back in.

Common questions

My device isn't listed. Check that it's connected and appears in Audio MIDI Setup (Window → Show MIDI Studio). The list updates automatically when devices are connected or removed.

My iPad doesn't show up. macOS only exposes an iPad as a MIDI device after you press Enable next to it in Audio MIDI Setup's audio device list.

The indicators don't light up. Make sure an input and an output are both selected, and that the message type you're sending isn't unchecked.

Privacy Policy

Midi Passthrough does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It makes no network connections and contains no analytics or advertising. The app saves only your selected input and output device names and your filter checkboxes, locally on your Mac. MIDI data passes through the app in memory and is never recorded or transmitted.
